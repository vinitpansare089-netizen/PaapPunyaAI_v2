TEACHING SUMMARY:
Krishna explains that whenever the restless and unsteady mind wanders, it should be brought back under control. The instruction recognizes that the mind may wander repeatedly and emphasizes returning it rather than giving up.

PRACTICAL INTERPRETATION:
During study, work or reflection, attention may drift. Notice the distraction and gently return to the task. Repeating this process is part of developing concentration; a wandering mind does not mean that practice has failed.
