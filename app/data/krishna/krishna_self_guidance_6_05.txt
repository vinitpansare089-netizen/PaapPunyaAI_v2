TEACHING SUMMARY:
Krishna teaches that a person should raise and strengthen oneself rather than allow oneself to decline. The mind can function as a friend when it is trained and directed well, but it can become an obstacle when it is uncontrolled.

PRACTICAL INTERPRETATION:
Build habits that support clear thinking and responsible action. Do not surrender every decision to an immediate impulse. Small repeated choices can strengthen or weaken self-discipline.
