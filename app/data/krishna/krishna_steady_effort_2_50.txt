TEACHING SUMMARY:
Bhagavad Gita 2.50 continues the discussion of wisdom in action. It presents yoga as skill or excellence in action, in the context of acting with disciplined understanding rather than being driven only by the expected fruits of action.

PRACTICAL INTERPRETATION:
Before acting, understand the situation, consider consequences and choose a responsible course. Skillful action combines thoughtful judgement with steady execution; it is not simply acting quickly or chasing a reward.
