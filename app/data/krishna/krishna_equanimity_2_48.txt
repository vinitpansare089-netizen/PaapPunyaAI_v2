TEACHING SUMMARY:
Krishna advises Arjuna to act with steadiness and without excessive attachment. Success and failure should not completely control the state of one's mind. The verse describes evenness of mind as an important aspect of yoga.

PRACTICAL INTERPRETATION:
Do your best after thoughtful preparation. If you succeed, remain humble; if you fail, learn and continue responsibly. Do not let either outcome define your entire worth.
