TEACHING SUMMARY:
Krishna tells Arjuna that his responsibility is to perform the action that is appropriate to him, rather than becoming fixated on controlling the results. He should not make reward the sole motive for action, and he should not use this teaching as an excuse to avoid action.

PRACTICAL INTERPRETATION:
When facing an important responsibility, focus on making a careful and ethical effort. Outcomes can depend on many factors outside one's control. Accepting that uncertainty does not mean abandoning responsibility.
