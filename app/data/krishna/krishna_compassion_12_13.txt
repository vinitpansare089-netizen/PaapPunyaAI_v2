TEACHING SUMMARY:
Bhagavad Gita 12.13 describes a person dear to Krishna as one who does not hate beings, is friendly and compassionate, is free from possessiveness and ego, remains balanced through pleasure and pain, and is forgiving.

PRACTICAL INTERPRETATION:
In disagreements, try to understand another person's situation without responding with hatred. Compassion does not require agreeing with every action or abandoning reasonable boundaries. It means treating others with humanity while acting responsibly.

This is a paraphrase of Bhagavad Gita 12.13. The practical note about boundaries is an application, not a direct quotation from the verse.
