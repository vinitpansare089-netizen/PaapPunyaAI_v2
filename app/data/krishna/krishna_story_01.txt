
Title: Arjuna's Dilemma and the Duty to Act

Source: Bhagavad Gita, Chapter 2 and Chapter 3
Content type: Learning summary, not a verbatim translation

Summary:

On the battlefield of Kurukshetra, Arjuna becomes
distressed about fighting against his relatives,
teachers and friends.

Krishna guides Arjuna to understand the importance
of duty, disciplined action and wisdom.

Krishna explains the principle of performing one's
duty without becoming attached to the fruits of
one's actions.

The teaching encourages a person to act responsibly
and focus on the right action rather than being
controlled by anxiety about the outcome.

This summary is a simplified learning resource.
It is not a direct quotation from the Bhagavad Gita.
